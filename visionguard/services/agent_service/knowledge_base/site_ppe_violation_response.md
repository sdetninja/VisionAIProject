note: Site-level practice built on OSHA 1910.132. Not a regulation; the safety officer should edit and approve it.

# Responding to missing PPE
When a worker is seen without required PPE (missing helmet, missing vest, missing gloves, missing goggles), the nearest supervisor checks the evidence, then asks the worker to stop the task and put on the required PPE before continuing. Replace damaged or missing PPE from site stock at no cost to the worker, OSHA 1910.132(h). Do not resume work until the PPE is worn correctly.

# Repeated PPE violations
Repeated missing PPE by the same person points to a training gap and requires retraining, OSHA 1910.132(f)(3). Repeated missing PPE in the same zone points to a hazard assessment, signage, PPE supply or comfort problem; review the zone's hazard assessment, 1910.132(d). Escalate a repeated pattern to the area supervisor and the safety officer.

# Recording PPE violations
Every confirmed PPE violation is recorded in the incident log with date, time, camera, zone, missing PPE and the corrective action taken. Rejected detections are recorded as false positives so the vision model can be retrained. Records are used to spot trends, not to blame individuals.
