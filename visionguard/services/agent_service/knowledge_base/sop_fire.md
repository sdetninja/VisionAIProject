source: SOP-FIRE-03
title: Fire and smoke response (SAMPLE)

# Fire detected
If fire is detected, sound the alarm, evacuate the zone and call the emergency response team. Do not rely on automated detection alone; the supervisor must verify and confirm.

# Smoke detected
Smoke detected may be steam or dust. The supervisor should verify visually, check for ignition sources and ventilate. If a welding arc or steam is the cause, record as false positive.

# Hot work
Welding and cutting areas require a fire watch and extinguisher within reach.
