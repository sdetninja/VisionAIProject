# Eye and face protection required (goggles, safety glasses)
Each affected employee must use appropriate eye or face protection (safety glasses, goggles or a face shield) when exposed to flying particles, molten metal, liquid chemicals, acids or caustic liquids, chemical gases or vapors, or harmful light radiation, 1910.133(a)(1). Where flying objects are a hazard, eye protection must have side protection, 1910.133(a)(2). Employees with prescription glasses use protection that includes the prescription or fits over the glasses, 1910.133(a)(3). A worker missing goggles or safety glasses at a station that requires eye protection is out of compliance.

# Welding filter lenses and eye protection standard
For welding, cutting and brazing, filter lenses must have a shade number suitable for the work, 1910.133(a)(5); for example shielded metal arc welding uses shade 7 to 11 depending on current, and torch soldering shade 2. Eye and face protection must meet ANSI/ISEA Z87.1-2010, Z87.1-2003 or Z87.1-1989 (R-1998), or be shown to be at least as effective, 1910.133(b).
