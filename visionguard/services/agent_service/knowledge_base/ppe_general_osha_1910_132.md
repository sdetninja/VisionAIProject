# PPE hazard assessment
The employer must assess the workplace for hazards that are present or likely to be present and that need personal protective equipment (PPE), 1910.132(d)(1). Where hazards exist, the employer selects PPE that protects against them, tells each affected employee which PPE was selected, and makes sure it fits each person. The assessment is certified in writing with the workplace, the person who certified it and the date, 1910.132(d)(2). A repeated missing PPE pattern in one zone is a reason to review that zone's hazard assessment.

# PPE provision, condition and cost
Protective equipment for the eyes, face, head, hands and body must be provided, used and kept in a sanitary and reliable condition wherever hazards require it, 1910.132(a). It must be of safe design for the work, 1910.132(c). If employees bring their own PPE, the employer is still responsible for its adequacy, maintenance and sanitation, 1910.132(b). Required PPE is provided at no cost to employees, with listed exceptions, 1910.132(h)(1).

# PPE training and retraining
Each employee who must use PPE is trained on when it is needed, which PPE is needed, how to put it on, take it off, adjust and wear it, its limits, and its care, maintenance, useful life and disposal, 1910.132(f)(1). Employees show they understand before working, 1910.132(f)(2). Retraining is required when an employee's use of PPE shows they have not kept the understanding or skill, for example repeated missing PPE, and when the workplace or the PPE type changes, 1910.132(f)(3).
