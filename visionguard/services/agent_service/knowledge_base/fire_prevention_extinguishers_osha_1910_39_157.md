# Fire prevention plan
The fire prevention plan lists the major fire hazards, how hazardous materials are handled and stored, the ignition sources and how they are controlled, and the fire protection equipment for each hazard, 1910.39(c)(1). It covers control of flammable and combustible waste, maintenance of safeguards on heat-producing equipment, and who is responsible for ignition sources and fuel hazards, 1910.39(c)(2)-(5). Employees are told about the fire hazards of their job when first assigned, 1910.39(d). Repeated fire or smoke alerts in a zone are a reason to review its fire hazards.

# Portable fire extinguishers
Extinguishers are mounted, located and marked so employees can reach them without risk of injury, 1910.157(c)(1). Travel distance to an extinguisher is 75 feet (22.9 m) or less for ordinary combustibles (Class A) and 50 feet (15.2 m) or less for flammable liquids (Class B), 1910.157(d)(2) and (d)(4). Extinguishers are checked visually every month and maintained every year, 1910.157(e)(2)-(3). Employees get extinguisher training when hired and at least once a year, 1910.157(g).
