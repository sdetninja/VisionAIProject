# Head protection required (helmet, hard hat)
Each affected employee must wear a protective helmet (hard hat) when working in areas where there is a potential for head injury from falling objects, 1910.135(a)(1). Employees working near exposed electrical conductors that could contact the head must wear helmets designed to reduce electrical shock, 1910.135(a)(2). A worker missing a helmet (no hard hat) in a zone that requires head protection is out of compliance until a helmet is worn.

# Helmet standard ANSI Z89.1, types and classes
Helmets must meet ANSI Z89.1-2009, -2003 or -1997, or be shown to be at least as effective, 1910.135(b). ANSI/ISEA Z89.1 types: Type I protects against impacts to the top of the head; Type II protects the top and sides. Electrical classes: Class G (general, tested at 2,200 volts), Class E (electrical, tested at 20,000 volts), Class C (conductive, no electrical protection). The class needed depends on the hazard assessment.

# Helmet inspection and replacement
Inspect the shell and suspension before each use. Remove from service a hard hat with cracks, dents, deep scratches, chalky or faded shell, or a damaged suspension, and any hard hat that has taken a significant impact, even with no visible damage. Follow the manufacturer's service life for shell and suspension. Wear it as designed: not backwards unless the manufacturer allows it, and nothing between the head and the suspension.
