# Respiratory protection (mask, respirator)
Harmful dusts, fumes, mists, gases and vapors are first controlled with engineering controls; respirators are used where those are not feasible or while they are being installed, 1910.134(a)(1). Where respirators are needed, the employer provides suitable ones and runs a written respiratory protection program with selection, medical evaluation, fit testing, training and maintenance, 1910.134(a)(2) and (c)(1). Tight-fitting respirators are fit tested before first use and at least once a year, 1910.134(f)(2). A worker missing a required mask or respirator is out of compliance. A camera cannot tell a dust mask from a fitted respirator, so a mask alert needs human review.
