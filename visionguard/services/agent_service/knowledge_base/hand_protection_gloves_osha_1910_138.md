# Hand protection required (gloves)
Employers must select and require employees to use appropriate hand protection (gloves) when hands are exposed to hazards such as skin absorption of harmful substances, severe cuts or lacerations, severe abrasions, punctures, chemical burns, thermal burns and harmful temperature extremes, 1910.138(a). A worker missing gloves (bare hands) at a station that requires hand protection is out of compliance.

# Glove selection
The choice of gloves is based on an evaluation of their performance for the tasks, the conditions, how long they are worn, and the hazards identified, 1910.138(b). One glove does not fit every hazard: cut-resistant gloves do not protect against chemicals, and chemical gloves must match the chemical. ANSI/ISEA 105 rates cut resistance from A1 (lowest) to A9 (highest), and also rates puncture, abrasion and heat resistance. Gloves near rotating machinery can be caught and pull the hand in, so the hazard assessment also says where gloves must not be worn.
