# Hot work fire precautions
Before welding or cutting, move fire hazards away from the work, or shield them with guards against heat, sparks and slag; if neither is possible, the hot work must not be done, 1910.252(a)(1). Suitable fire extinguishing equipment is kept ready for instant use, 1910.252(a)(2)(ii). The person who authorises the work inspects the area first and sets the precautions, preferably in a written hot work permit, 1910.252(a)(2)(iv).

# Fire watch for welding and cutting
A fire watch is required when combustibles are within 35 feet (about 11 m) of the work, when sparks could reach combustibles further away, through wall or floor openings, or behind metal walls, 1910.252(a)(2)(iii)(A). The fire watch has extinguishing equipment, is trained to use it and to sound the alarm, and stays for at least 30 minutes after the work ends, 1910.252(a)(2)(iii)(B). Hot work is not allowed where sprinklers are out of service, in explosive atmospheres or near large amounts of easily ignited material, 1910.252(a)(2)(vi).
