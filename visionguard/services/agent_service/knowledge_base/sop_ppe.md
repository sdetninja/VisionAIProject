# Head protection
Hard hats must be worn in all production floor zones and loading areas. A worker found without a helmet must be asked to stop work and put on approved head protection before continuing. Repeated missing helmet violations are escalated to the area supervisor.

# High-visibility vests
High-visibility vests are required wherever forklifts or trucks operate. Missing vest in forklift lane zones must be corrected immediately by the supervisor.

# Gloves and eye protection
Gloves and goggles are required at welding, grinding and chemical handling stations. Missing goggles or gloves at these stations requires work stoppage until protection is worn.

# Documentation
Every confirmed PPE violation is recorded in the incident log with date, zone and corrective action taken.
