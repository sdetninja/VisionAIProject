title: Restricted area access (SAMPLE)

# Restricted area entry
Only authorised personnel may enter restricted areas such as press machines and chemical stores. An unauthorised entry into a restricted area must be stopped by the nearest supervisor and logged as a restricted area entry incident.

# Forklift lanes
Pedestrians must use marked walkways. Entering a forklift lane on foot is a restricted area entry; the forklift operator must stop and the supervisor must escort the person out.
